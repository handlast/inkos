## Anti-AI Tell Gate

Before submitting, remove generated-text texture at the source:

- No Expository report tone: do not explain the scene with "this means", "in essence", "the key is"; make the object, action, pressure, reaction, or dialogue carry the fact.
- No abstract conclusion beats: do not tell readers the character "realized the truth" unless a decision, cost, physical reaction, or relationship shift makes that truth visible.
- No telegraph paragraphing: do not stack one-line action beats. Merge action + observation + reaction into fuller paragraphs; save one-line paragraphs for real impact.
- No Dialogue voice collapse / interchangeable cold dialogue: every speaking character needs at least one voice fingerprint from role cards, profession, desire, fear, relationship pressure, or refusal style.
- No formulaic transition glue: pivot through timing, movement, blocking, interruption, or a changed choice instead of repeating "however/meanwhile/still".

### Show Don't Tell — Hard Rules

NEVER write these patterns. Replace them with concrete physical evidence:

FORBIDDEN psychological summary: "he knew all too well", "she was well aware", "he understood deeply", "he realized acutely" → SHOW the knowledge through a decision, a flinch, a prepared action, or a changed tactic.

FORBIDDEN emotional labeling: "he felt angry", "she was filled with sorrow", "a wave of anxiety washed over him" → SHOW the emotion through clenched fists, a cracking voice, an inability to eat, a reckless choice.

FORBIDDEN fate/destiny narration: "wheels of fate began to turn", "fate had other plans", "destiny brought them together" → SHOW the convergence through coincidences the character notices, choices that inadvertently intersect, or information that arrives at the worst moment.

FORBIDDEN micro-expression spam: "his lips curved slightly", "her eyes flashed with surprise", "a cold light flickered in his gaze" → REPLACE with at most ONE per scene; use body language that costs something (standing up too fast, dropping what they're holding, going silent for three beats).

FORBIDDEN collective reaction: "everyone gasped", "the crowd fell silent", "all eyes turned to him" → SHOW one specific person's reaction with a name and a concrete action.
